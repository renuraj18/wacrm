Privacy Policy

Shanthi Boutique · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information Shanthi Boutique (“we”, “us”) collects when you message us on WhatsApp or otherwise contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to talk with customers.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name you have set in WhatsApp.
  • Messages: the text, photos, voice notes, documents and other media you send us, and the replies we send you.
  • Order and enquiry details: products you ask about or buy, delivery details you share, and notes our team adds to help serve you.
  • Delivery information: whether our messages were delivered and read, as reported by WhatsApp.

We do not collect your WhatsApp contacts list, location, or any information you have not chosen to share with us.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To process orders, arrange delivery and send order updates.
  • To send you offers or updates you have agreed to receive. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep a record of our conversations so any member of our team can help you.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which delivers messages between you and us. Meta’s own handling of data is described in the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store our customer records securely on our behalf.
  • AI service providers, if we use an AI assistant to help draft replies. Only the conversation needed to draft a reply is sent, and it is not used to sell or advertise to you.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation and order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it.

How we protect it

Our customer records are stored with access controls so only authorised team members can see them. Credentials used to connect to WhatsApp are encrypted. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop marketing messages: reply STOP on WhatsApp, or ask us to stop.
  • Access or correct: ask us what information we hold about you, or to correct it.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your information, as described below.

Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, send a WhatsApp message to our business number saying “Delete my data”, or email renuraj18@gmail.com with the phone number you used to contact us. We will delete your contact record and conversation history within 30 days and confirm when it is done, except for anything we must keep by law (for example, invoices).

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
